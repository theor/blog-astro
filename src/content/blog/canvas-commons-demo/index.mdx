A test post for the Canvas Commons integration. The animation below is a
`src/animations/hello.project.ts` project, bundled out-of-process by the integration and
played by `@canvas-commons/player`. Click it to play.

<Animation src="hello" caption="hello.project.ts - click to play" />

The bundle is only fetched once the animation gets close to the viewport, so a post can
carry several of them without paying for any it never shows. `eager` opts out of that,
and `auto` starts playback as soon as the animation is ready:

<Animation src="animations/hello" auto="hover" quality={0.5} caption="Same project, playing on hover at half resolution" />
